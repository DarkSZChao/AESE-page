Dr Di Wu

Di Wu is currently an Associate Professor at Hunan University-HNU and until 2015 was  a Research Associate at Imperial College London and Intel Collaborative Research Institute for Sustainable Connected Cites (ICRI-Cities). He is also an Adjunct Researcher at the University of California Transportation Center, USA. He received the Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from the University of California, Irvine, USA in 2013. He was a Staff Research Associate at the University of California, Irvine, USA; a Student Research Associate at the Stanford Research Institute, Menlo Park, USA; a Visiting Researcher at IBM Research, China; and a Staff Member at the Department of Land and Resources of Hunan Province, China. He is a member of IEEE and ACM.

Research Interests:

  • Wireless Networks and Mobile Computing
  • Internet-of-Things and Cyber-Physical Systems
  • Smart Cities and Big Data

Research Projects at ICRI:

  • Mobility Management in Software-Defined IoT
  • Mobile Networking in the London Underground

  • Deep Learning for Mobile Data
