Dr Anqi Fu

Research Associate

Biography

Anqi Fu is currently working as a postdoctoral research associate in the AESE group at Imperial College London and The Alan Turing Institute on Data Centric Engineering Programme. He received his Ph.D. in Control Systems from Delft University of Technology in February 2018 where he worked on “Efficient wireless networked control. Towards practical event-triggered implementations.” (ISBN 9789461868947) His research interests include wireless networked control systems, cyber-physical system security, and hybrid systems.
