Dr Roman Kolcun

Visiting Research Assistant

Biography

He graduated from Imperial College London in 2010 and after a year spent in industry he decided to pursue a PhD degree at the very same university. His interests include, but are not limited to wireless sensor networks, large scale distributed systems, web programming, distributed databases, and functional programming.

In his PhD he focuses on possibilities how to push the computation from the base-station into the wireless sensor network.
