Heba Abdelnasser Awad

PhD Student

Heba is a Ph.D. student in AESE group in Department of Computing at Imperial College London. Her research interests include mobile and ubiquitous computing, robotics monitoring, human gesture recognition and localization.

She received the B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in computer science from Alexandria University, Egypt in 2013 and 2018, respectively. She was a research assistant in the Wireless Research Centre at Alexandria University, Egypt and a team member in the Networking System Lab at Carnegie Melon University Qatar from 2013 to 2018.
