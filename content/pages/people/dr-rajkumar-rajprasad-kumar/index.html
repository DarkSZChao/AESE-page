Biography 

Dr. Rajprasad completed his PhD  from the University of Nottingham in 2011. He is currently Research Fellow at the Adaptive Emergent Systems Engineering (AESE) group at Imperial College. His research work here is primarily focused on Sensors and Machine learning. The areas of application are in the fields of lift fault detection and rainfall prediction. He has 15 years of previous experience in the application of machine learning in various applications both in academic and consultancy roles in the fields of oil and gas, renewable energy, electric vehicles, electrical load prediction, supercapacitors and fault prediction.
