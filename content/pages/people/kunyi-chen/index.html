Ms Kunyi Chen

Visiting PhD Student

She is currently a visiting PhD student under the supervision of Prof. Julie A. McCann in the AESE group. She received her B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2016. Now she is working towards a PhD degree in Computer Science at the same university.

Her work focuses on the following topics:

  1. Data transmission scheduling in Energy-Harvesting Wireless Sensor Network.
  2. In-network data processing in WSN/IoT. Here, in-network query, data aggregation, and data cleaning techniques are included.

 

Publications:

[1] Kunyi Chen, Hong Gao, Zhipeng Cai, Quan Chen, Jianzhong Li: Distributed Energy-Adaptive Aggregation Scheduling with Coverage Guarantee For Battery-Free Wireless Sensor Networks. INFOCOM 2019: 1018-1026

[2] Kunyi Chen, Siyao Cheng, Hong Gao, Jianzhong Li: A Functional Analysis Based Strategy for Preserving Location Privacy. BigCom 2018: 120-125
