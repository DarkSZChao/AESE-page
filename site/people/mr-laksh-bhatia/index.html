Mr Laksh Bhatia

Research Assistant, PhD Student

Biography

Laksh Bhatia is a PhD student and Research Assistant advised by Prof. Julie McCann in AESE Group of Department of Computing at Imperial College London. His research focuses on aspects of wireless communication systems with applications in cyber-physical systems and internet of things.

In 2017, He received a Dual MSc degree in Embedded Systems and Minor in Innovation and Enterprenurship from Technical University of Berlin and Aalto University. He received a BE degree from Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani in 2015 in Electrical and Electronics engineering.
