Dr Attique Dawood

Research Associate

Computing Department

Biobraphy

Attique Dawood received his PhD from Imperial College London in 2020. His research interests are numerical modelling of resonant structures at microwave and terahertz frequencies, characterisation of novel 3-D printed materials and radio-frequency (RF) sensing. He is currently working on novel RF sensors for detecting mechanical and electrical activity.
